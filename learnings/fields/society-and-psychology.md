# Society, culture and psychology

Apple categories: Society & Culture.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Peer-reviewed social science; large surveys from official statistics or established research bodies.
- **Watch for:** The replication crisis in psychology, correlation versus cause, one-country samples. Say who was studied.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Mental-health advice, which is handled as health.
