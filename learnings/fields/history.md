# History

Apple categories: History.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Scholarly books and journal articles, primary sources, reputable reference works.
- **Watch for:** Popular myths and misquotes. Say where historians disagree and why. Dates and the spelling of names.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Topics prone to denial: atrocity and genocide denial is never treated as a legitimate side.
