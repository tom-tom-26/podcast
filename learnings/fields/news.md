# News and current events

Apple categories: News.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Primary document (statement, ruling, filing, dataset) plus 2 independent reputable outlets per key claim.
- **Watch for:** Single-source scoops and fast-moving facts. Say the date aloud ("as of the twenty-ninth of October"). Keep reported fact, open dispute and analysis clearly separate.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Ongoing legal cases; unconfirmed casualty figures.
