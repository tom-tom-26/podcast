# True crime and law

Apple categories: True Crime.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Court records, judgments, official inquiry reports, established long-form journalism.
- **Watch for:** Presumption of innocence, living people and victims. Never speculate on guilt beyond the record. Defamation.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Unresolved cases; named living suspects.
