# Science, space and nature

Apple categories: Science.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Reviews and meta-analyses, major scientific bodies, mission and agency data.
- **Watch for:** Preprints, single striking studies, press-release hype. Say when something is a hypothesis.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Rarely; environmental-health claims are handled as health.
