# Politics and government

Apple categories: Government, News.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Legislation, official records, election data and polls from established pollsters, nonpartisan analysts.
- **Watch for:** Partisan framing. Give contested claims both sides fairly. Polls are snapshots with margins of error.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Elections in progress; always label analysis versus fact.
