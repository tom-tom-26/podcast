# Health, fitness and nutrition

Apple categories: Health & Fitness.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Systematic reviews and meta-analyses, RCTs, major guidelines.
- **Watch for:** Name animal and cell studies as such. Absolute versus relative risk. Supplement-industry funding.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Doses, fasting, drugs, supplements, medical conditions.
