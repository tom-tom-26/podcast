# Sport

Apple categories: Sports.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** Governing-body statistics and rules, established stats providers, sports-science studies for training claims.
- **Watch for:** Stat definitions differ between sources. Small-sample "hot streaks". Transfer and injury rumours are not facts.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Training or injury advice, which is handled as health.
