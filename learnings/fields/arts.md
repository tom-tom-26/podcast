# Arts, music, film and books

Apple categories: Arts, Music, TV & Film.

## Profile

- **Strongest evidence:** The works themselves, artist interviews, reputable criticism and scholarship, industry data.
- **Watch for:** Separate fact (dates, sales, history) from taste. Critics disagree. Warn about spoilers up front.
- **Higher-risk flag when:** Rarely.
