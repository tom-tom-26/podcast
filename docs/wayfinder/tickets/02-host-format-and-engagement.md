## Question

Is there scientific benefit to multiple hosts (conversation/dialogue) versus a single host for learning and retention? Separately, what makes educational podcast hosts engaging in style (pacing, storytelling, analogies, structure, vocal delivery)? Include what makes Huberman's structure work and how a single non-robotic host can achieve it.

## Resolution

Closed 2026-10-29. Findings in `research/02-host-format-and-engagement.md`. Single host recommended: the dialogue effect (Chi et al. 2017) is fragile, failed to replicate, and comes from tutor-tutee exchanges in video, not two experts chatting. A conversational single narrator captures much of the benefit (personalization/voice principles, small effects). No podcast-specific evidence either way. File ends with 13 style rules (signposts, simulating listener questions inside one voice, writing for the ear, ~150-180 wpm). Evidence read from abstracts/summaries; Huberman structure mapping is the agent's own reading of one episode. Whether TTS can deliver the prosody rules depends on the TTS ticket.
